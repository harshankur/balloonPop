Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Balloon Pop is a free game I, Harsh Ankur, built and run. It is available as a website at balloonpop.harshankur.com and as an Android app that shows that same website. This policy covers both.

In short: there are no accounts, no ads, and no cookies. Your best score and sound setting stay on your device. The site counts visits in a privacy-friendly way that cannot identify you, and a few services that deliver the page see your IP address, as any website does.

What stays on your device

The game saves two small settings in your browser's local storage, and they never leave your device:

  • bp.best, your best score.
  • bp.sound, whether sound is on or off.

Clearing your browser's site data for Balloon Pop removes them. Earlier versions of the game kept your best score in two cookies instead (firstVisit and highestPoppedBalloons). The current version reads that old score once, to carry it over, and sets no cookies of its own. Any old cookies stay on your device until you clear them.

What is counted when you visit

The published website counts visits with viewcounter, a view counter I run on my own server. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and sends no identifier. A copy of the game that you download or run on your own computer sends nothing to the counter.

When you open the game, it records:

  • the page you opened, its title, and a rough screen size (small, medium or large);
  • the website you came from, as a domain and path only, and any campaign tags (utm_) on the link;
  • how long the page was visible;
  • the domain, and nothing more, of any link to another site that you click.

To work out your country, browser and operating system, the server briefly sees your IP address and browser details. It masks the end of the IP address before storing anything, and it does not keep your raw IP address or your browser details. To tell repeat visits apart, it keeps a one-way hash that is salted differently every day, so it cannot follow you from one day to the next. I use these records to see how many people play and from which countries.

The Android app shows this same website, so its visits are counted in the same way. If you have questions about these records, contact me.

Services that deliver the game

These services receive your IP address and basic browser details when your device loads the game, the same as for any website. The game's typeface is part of the game itself, so it is not fetched from anyone else:

  • Netlify and Cloudflare host the site and deliver it to you. See the Netlify and Cloudflare privacy policies.

I do not use advertising, social media widgets or any analytics service other than the view counter above. The Android app asks only for permission to use the internet and check your connection. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or files.

Links and sharing

The game links to other sites, and I do not control them. They only learn about you if you choose to open them, and each has its own policy:

  • Buy Me a Coffee, if you tap "Buy me a coffee".
  • X, if you tap "Post" to share your score. Your score message, the game's link and a hashtag are passed to X, and nothing else.
  • harshankur.com, my own site, from the credit at the bottom of the game.

"Share" opens your device's share menu, and "Copy link" puts the message on your clipboard. Neither sends anything to me.

Children

The game does not ask for any personal information, so it does not knowingly collect any from anyone, including children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and think a child has given me personal information, contact me and I will remove it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. I keep the data I do collect to the minimum described here. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the date at the top. Please check back from time to time.

Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions about this policy, contact me at hello@harshankur.com.
